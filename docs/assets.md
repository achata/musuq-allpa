## Prompt de extracción utilizado

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the attached local poster image. Extract ONLY the central dark forest-green Neugrac mascot: the happy onion/seed character with raised arms, overalls, feet, and one tall leaf growing from its head. Create a clean icon cutout on a genuinely transparent background, centered in a square canvas. Preserve the exact original dark green line art, silhouette, relative proportions, expression, overalls, leaf and original colors. Keep the light ivory interior shapes of the character opaque, but make all space outside its outline transparent, including the open spaces between arms and body and around the leaf. Remove all poster text, the 'MUSUQ ALLPA' wordmark, subtitle, header, border leaves, Cultivar button, mountains and landscape. Do not redesign, do not invent details, do not add text, no shadows, no gradients, no new elements. Fit the extracted mascot large in the square with a small clean margin on every side. The output will be the app's browser tab favicon.
